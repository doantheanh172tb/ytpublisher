MPT Campaigns – YouTube Publisher

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What this app does

MPT Campaigns is an internal automation tool. It produces short news and educational videos and uploads them to YouTube channels that are owned and operated by the app's developer, using the YouTube Data API v3.

Permissions requested

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos to the authorized channel.
  • youtube.readonly – to read the channel's own video list and basic statistics (views, likes, comments) so the tool can avoid duplicate uploads and report performance.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in to it.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
