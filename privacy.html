Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy describes how MPT Campaigns – YouTube Publisher ("the app") handles information when the channel owner authorizes it with a Google account.

1. Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by its developer to publish videos to YouTube channels the developer owns. It is not offered to other users.

2. Information the app accesses

Through Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API, the app accesses:

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token for the authorized YouTube channel.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) and the channel's own uploaded videos.
  • Public statistics for those videos (views, likes, comment counts).

The app does not request or read your Gmail, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

3. How the information is used

  • To upload videos produced by the app to the authorized channel.
  • To check which videos have already been uploaded and avoid duplicates.
  • To produce internal performance reports for the channel owner.

4. Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the developer's own computer. They are not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and they are not used for advertising.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time and become unusable once access is revoked.

6. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest revision.
